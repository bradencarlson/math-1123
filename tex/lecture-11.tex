\content{
        \begin{example} Pierce and Buzz are splitting a foot-long sandwich
        worth \$20, which is half chicken and half turkey. Buzz likes turkey 3
        times as much as he likes chicken, and Pierce likes turkey 2 times as
        much as he likes chicken. They decide to use the divider chooser method
        to cut the sandwich and split it between the two of them. 
        \begin{enumerate}
        \item If Buzz is the
        divider, where does he cut the sandwich (remember that only a single vertical
        slice is allowed)? 
        \item Which piece does Pierce choose? 
        \item Determine how much each piece is worth to their
        recipients.
        \end{enumerate}
        \end{example}
